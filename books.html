श्रीमद्भगवद्गीता • अध्याय २ Sankhya Yoga
श्लोक ४७ (Verse 47)

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥

"karmaṇy-evādhikāras te mā phaleṣhu kadāchana
mā karma-phala-hetur bhūr mā te saṅgo ’stvakarmaṇi"

Literal Meaning:

Thy right is to work only, but never to its fruits; let not the fruit of action be thy motive, nor let thy attachment be to inaction.

42 Swami Chidbhavananda Commentary
Discourse Analysis Selfless Action

Exposition by Swami Chidbhavananda:

This famous verse is the bedrock of Karma Yoga. The Lord clarifies that man holds jurisdiction solely over deliberate effort, never over eventual repercussions. Desiring outcomes distracts mental concentration and breeds anxiety.

Equally dangerous is the temptation towards lethargy (*akarmaṇi*). Often people argue: "If I cannot claim the harvest, why should I till the soil?" Sri Krishna warns Arjuna against this spiritual stagnation. Work performed as selfless worship (*Ishwara Arpana*) purifies the chitta, rendering it receptive to the realization of the Atman.

Sri Ramakrishna's Analogy:

"The maidservant attends to all duties in the master's house with devoted love, feeding the children tenderly, yet she knows in her heart that none of these belong to her."
